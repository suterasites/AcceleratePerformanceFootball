02 · What's included

Periodised, not random.

13 weeks of weekly sessions is enough time to build a real periodisation plan - high-volume technique early, match-pressure work mid-block, sharpening before retest. Each week builds on the last with a clear arc.

  • 01

    Full assessment + written baseline

    Week 1-2 is a deep dive into technical, tactical, and physical metrics. You leave with a written baseline that the rest of the block measures against.

  • 02

    Periodised 13-week plan

    Build, sharpen, retest. Each phase has a different drill density, different intensity, different focus. This is what makes 13 weeks more than 13 disconnected sessions.

  • 03

    Mid-block reassessment

    Week 7 we stop and measure. If something's working, we double down. If something's not, we course-correct before the back half of the block.

  • 04

    Position-specific work

    A striker doesn't need the same drills as a centre-back. The block plan adapts to position - finishing scenarios for forwards, defensive recovery for backs, distribution for wide players.

  • 05

    Week 13 retest + roadmap

    Same drills, same metrics as week 1. Tangible, measurable change. Then we map what's next - another Game Changer, off-season conditioning, or step up to the Total 90.

03 · How the 13 weeks play out

Four phases. One arc.

Weeks

01-02

Baseline phase

Technical, tactical, physical screen. Position read. Written assessment. You finish week 2 knowing exactly where you sit and what the block will target.

Weeks

03-07

Build phase

High-volume technique. Targeted drilling of the weaknesses surfaced in baseline. Physical conditioning ramps. This is the bedrock - it isn't flashy, but it's where everything else gets built on.

Week

07

Mid-block reassessment

Halfway checkpoint. We measure against the baseline. Plan adjusts for the back half if something needs more or less attention.

Weeks

08-12

Sharpen phase

Match-pressure scenarios. The same techniques now under defender, time, and decision pressure. This is where game-day transfer happens - players start seeing the work show up in their actual matches.

Week

13

Retest + roadmap

Same drills, same metrics as week 1. Tangible, measurable change. Then we talk about what's next - another Game Changer for depth, or step up to the Total 90 for full-season structure.

04 · Common questions

Before you book.

What does 13 weeks actually look like?
One 60-minute session per week across 13 weeks. The block is periodised - weeks 1-2 are assessment and baseline, weeks 3-8 are build phase (high-volume technique + conditioning), weeks 9-12 are sharpen phase (match-pressure scenarios), and week 13 is retest against the baseline.
What if I miss a week?
You can carry missed sessions forward by up to 4 weeks past the original block end date. If you're going to miss more than 2 sessions, let us know in advance and we'll reshape the periodisation so you don't lose the progression - or pause the block and resume it.
Can I extend after 13 weeks?
Yes. Most Game Changer players roll into either another 13-week block or step up to the 26-week Total 90. By week 13's retest we have a clear picture of what's next.
Is the Game Changer enough for serious comp prep?
For most amateur and junior comps, yes - 13 weeks of periodised work plus club training is a real preparation block. For elite-level prep (state rep, NPL, scholarship trials), pair the Game Changer with off-season conditioning the season before, or step up to the Total 90 for full-year structure.
What's the discount tier for siblings or West Point SC?
Siblings training together and registered West Point Soccer Club players pay $55 per session on a 5-pack at $715 total (compared to standard $60/session over 13 weeks at $780 total). The discount tier is a shorter, denser block - plan a follow-up if you want full half-season depth.
