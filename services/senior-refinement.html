Senior Refinement

Refine what makes you dangerous.

For experienced players who already understand the game and want to level up.

01

Assessment

Evaluating technical ability, decision-making, and physical conditioning. Gives you a clear picture of where you sit and sets realistic targets.

02

Technical Refinement

Advanced drills on ball control, passing accuracy, and tactical awareness. Sharpening existing skills rather than teaching new ones. Each session builds on your existing foundation.

03

Game Awareness

Tactical positioning, reading the field, anticipating movement, executing under pressure. This is what separates good players from elite players.

04

Complete Game

Match-ready conditioning for in-season and off-season fitness, mental resilience under pressure, and injury prevention strategies. Building the complete package.

04 · Who it's for

Players past the basics.

Senior-grade players

State league, NPL, amateur, social comp. Anyone playing senior football who wants to lift the technical ceiling.

Pre-season & trials

Players approaching trial windows or club pre-seasons who need to peak on demand.

Position-specific work

Strikers, midfielders, defenders, goalkeepers. Drills are built around what your position actually demands.

Returning seniors

Players coming back to senior football after a break or a step down. Get back to match standard, then push past it.

05 · Inside a 60-minute session

How the hour breaks down.

Min 0-10

Activation

Dynamic prep loaded for the day's main work. Position-specific movement primers - quick feet for midfielders, change-of-direction for defenders, shoulder-loading for GKs.

Min 10-35

Technical refinement

Position-specific drills. Strikers finish, midfielders scan, defenders read, GKs distribute. Refining what already works rather than teaching from scratch.

Min 35-50

Pressure work

Small-sided constraints, restricted touches, decision-under-fatigue. Building habits that hold up when the game gets ugly.

Min 50-60

Match-pace conditioning

Sport-specific loading. Builds the engine that holds the full 90 - aerobic capacity layered with the repeated-sprint work that decides games.

07 · Common questions

Quick answers.

What level of player do you work with?
Anyone from grade-club seniors to state league. Sessions adapt to the player's current ability - we assess on the first session and build from there. The drill complexity and conditioning load scale to where you are.
Can you work on position-specific skills?
Yes. Drills are tailored to your position. Strikers do finishing under pressure, midfielders work on scanning and first-touch in tight spaces, defenders drill reading-and-recovery, goalkeepers work shot-stopping and distribution.
Do you work with players approaching trial windows?
Yes. We work with players approaching trials on what selectors look for - technical proficiency at speed, decision-making, off-ball movement. Sessions in the lead-up tighten the specific skills that show up in the test.
How long until I see improvement?
Most players notice technical sharpness within 4-6 sessions. Bigger shifts - tactical reads, in-game conditioning, composure under pressure - typically settle in across a full 13-week block.
Is this 1-on-1 or group?
Both. The 5-pack Tune Up runs as private 1-on-1 ($500). The Game Changer (13-week) and Total 90 (26-week) run as Small Unit (2-6 players) where players push each other through the drills.
