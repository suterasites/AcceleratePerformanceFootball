## Notes on existing copy

- Body copy includes "125+ athletes trained", "92% progression rate", "4+ years in business" - these are owner-stated and may stay in body, but must NOT enter schema unless objectively verifiable on a single platform.
- Pricing page has known inconsistencies between standard and discounted packages (Game Changer: 5-pack listed as 13-week block; Total 90: 13-week listed as 26-week). Out of scope for SEO pass; flag to client separately.

- **Phase 3b (today):** Bump sitemap.xml lastmod to 2026-05-08.
- **Phase 3c (today):** Commit + push. Cloudflare Pages auto-deploys.
- **Phase 2 (backlog):** Voice files (voice/stats/opinions/stories/humour). Source from existing site copy + Chris's coaching philosophy. Best done after a 30-min call with Chris.
- **Phase 4 (backlog):** Content expansion on the 4 service detail pages (currently 445-480 lines vs SERP norms). Add FAQs from "People Also Ask" for "private football coaching Melbourne" / "soccer coaching West Melbourne". Internal-link service pages to each other and to pricing.
