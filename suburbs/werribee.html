03 · The Werribee football scene

Scale. Diversity. Football culture.

Werribee runs one of the densest football scenes in Melbourne's west - junior and senior, across a deeply multicultural community where football is taken seriously. European, African, South American, and South Asian families all serious about player development. Players come through to us regardless of which club they're currently with.

The 12-15 minute drive up the Princes Freeway means most Werribee players slot training into a weeknight after school or work without disruption. The freeway run is direct so it stays predictable. Sibling pair bookings from Werribee families are common, taking the discounted Small Unit rate of $55 per session.

04 · Common questions from Werribee

Quick answers.

How long is the drive from Werribee to West Point SC?
Around 12-15 minutes via the Princes Freeway eastbound. Direct freeway run, free on-site parking when you arrive. Most Werribee families fit a session into a weeknight without rearranging dinner.
Can I train with you alongside my regular club?
Yes. We don't replace club training, we run alongside it. Players from any local club are welcome - Werribee has the deepest football catchment in our service area.
Is the coaching pitched at junior or senior level?
Both. We coach juniors from age 6 through to senior-league players. Werribee's scale means we see everything from young juniors stepping into rep selection to senior players prepping for higher-grade trials. Junior Prep and Senior Refinement are both heavily booked from this suburb.
Do sibling discounts apply for Werribee families?
Yes. Siblings booking Small Unit sessions together pay the discounted rate - $55/session on the Tune Up, $55/session on the Game Changer 5-pack, and $50/session on the Total 90 13-week block.
