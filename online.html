05 · Common questions

Quick answers.

What is APF Online?
An online training membership from Accelerate Performance Football. You get a growing library of drills, step-by-step technique tutorials, and athlete nutrition guidance you can follow from anywhere, all in one place. It brings the same methodology Chris uses in person to your phone.
Who is APF Online for?
Players who want a real training structure instead of guesswork, juniors building proper technique from the start, and coaches or parents who want professional drills to run at home. Ability and commitment matter more than age.
Do I need any equipment to start?
A ball and a bit of space is enough to get going. Most drills need nothing more than that, and each one tells you exactly what it requires before you start.
How is this different from in-person coaching?
In-person coaching is 1-on-1 or small-group sessions in West Melbourne with live feedback from a coach. APF Online is self-guided - the same drills, progressions, and standards, available anywhere and any time, at a fraction of the cost. Many players use both. See our in-person programs.
Is there a free trial and can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can start a free trial and explore the full platform before paying anything. After that it's a month-to-month membership at $6 per month that you can cancel whenever you like.
